About

I am Fatih Gurcaglar, a data and ML engineer in Sydney. I build SMSF Core, record-keeping software for self managed superannuation fund trustees, with a development process that runs mostly on autonomous coding agents working inside explicit safety boundaries.

This site is where I write about that process: what agents can safely own, what they never should, and the mechanisms that make the difference enforceable rather than aspirational.

The mark in the header is a Swiss Army knife. A manager once described me as one, and it stuck: one operator, many tools, most of them folded away until a job needs them.
